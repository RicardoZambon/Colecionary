# Brand Identity

Translated and restructured from the Colecionary brand manual (v1.0, May/2026),
sections 1–4. This document covers brand strategy and the logo system. For colors,
typography, and components see [`design-system.md`](design-system.md).

> **Chosen direction: A+B — premium inventory + geek showcase.** The brand must
> look organized, trustworthy, and SaaS-like, yet carry enough visual personality
> to speak to geek collectors and enthusiasts of personal items.

---

## 1. Brand overview

This identity guides the final logo, the web interface, communication materials,
landing pages, sales decks, emails, social pieces, and future mobile experiences.

### Executive summary

| Element | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Colecionary |
| **Category** | SaaS for control, organization, and digital showcasing of personal collections |
| **Primary tagline** | _Sua coleção, organizada do seu jeito._ (Your collection, organized your way.) |
| **Personality** | Modern, organized, subtly geek, trustworthy, lightly premium |
| **Visual base** | Dark-first; purple as primary, cyan as technology, gold as rare/premium |
| **First channel** | Web app, evolving to mobile later |
| **Initial focus** | Item registration, photos, categories, estimated value, wishlist, public collections |
| **Product future** | Trades/sales, community, statistics, rarity, conservation state, QR Code/barcode, automatic search |

## 2. Strategy & positioning

### 2.1 Brand essence

Colecionary exists to turn personal collections into **organized, beautiful, easy
-to-browse digital archives**. The brand must **not** feel like a spreadsheet. It
should convey that every item has a place, a story, value, and a spotlight.

- **Promise:** help the user organize, visualize, and value their collection.
- **Functional benefit:** control of items, photos, categories, value, and wishlist.
- **Emotional benefit:** the pleasure of seeing the collection well presented, like
  a personal showcase.
- **Visual differentiator:** modern SaaS with an elegant geek atmosphere, without
  depending on any specific franchise, game, or category.

### 2.2 Audience & scope

The identity must **not** be limited to cards, action figures, or manga. The
audience spans collectors of pop culture, technology, books, comics, games,
hardware, consoles, rare items, nostalgic objects, and any personal collection
that needs organizing.

| Profile | Need | Brand response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Casual collector** | Register items and photos without complexity | Simple interface, visual cards, fast registration |
| **Organized collector** | Control categories, state, value, history | Trustworthy design system, clear filters, well-structured data |
| **Geek collector** | Feel personality and visual pleasure | Dark mode, subtle glows, rarity, badges, showcase language |
| **Future community user** | Share collection and discover items | Public collections with elegant, social presentation |

### 2.3 Verbal territory

- **Use:** collection, archive (_acervo_), showcase (_vitrine_), items, rarity,
  state, value, categories, wishlist, catalog, "digital shelf" (_prateleira
  digital_).
- **Avoid** excessive corporate terms such as _ativos_ (assets), _patrimônio_,
  _inventário patrimonial_, or _gestão de bens_ — except in administrative areas.
- **Avoid** language that is too childish, too meme-heavy, or slang that ages
  quickly.

See [`voice-and-tone.md`](voice-and-tone.md) for full copy guidelines.

## 3. Visual identity concept

The identity must unite two worlds: the **trust of a SaaS product** and the
**visual charm of a geek collection**. The result should be clean, scalable, and
full of "collectible" details.

### 3.1 Visual pillars

| Pillar | How it shows up visually | What to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Organization** | Grids, cards, shelves, clear hierarchy, predictable filters | Chaotic interfaces, too many simultaneous colors, misaligned cards |
| **Showcase** (_Vitrine_) | Large photos, item spotlights, beautiful public collections | Purely tabular screens with no visual appeal |
| **Subtle geek** | Glows, rarity badges, dark mode, purple/cyan accents | Aggressive gamer aesthetic, childish, or tied to a specific franchise |
| **Accessible premium** | Moderate gold, soft shadows, sophisticated dark tones | Exaggerated luxury, absolute black, heavy gradients |

### 3.2 Symbol concept

The recommended symbol combines a **minimalist box/shelf**, organized items, and a
**glow highlight** representing a rare item. It must work as the app icon, favicon,
social avatar, and an in-interface visual seal.

## 4. Logo & signature system

### 4.1 Required structure

The logo is composed of **symbol + wordmark**. The symbol guarantees recognition
in small spaces; the wordmark sustains the brand in institutional and commercial
materials.

- **Primary signature:** symbol on the left + "Colecionary" on the right.
- **Vertical signature:** symbol above + "Colecionary" below — for covers and
  centered layouts.
- **Symbol only:** favicon, app icon, avatar, loading, seal, compact buttons.
- **Wordmark only:** allowed only where the symbol already appears nearby, or
  under extreme width constraints.

### 4.2 Building the symbol

| Element | Meaning | Visual rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rounded box/shelf | Organization of the collection | Simple base shape, rounded corners, no excess detail |
| Inner items | Variety of collections | Generic shapes; never anything too item-specific |
| Highlighted central item | Value, rarity, pride | May use a cyan outline or purple fill |
| Glow/star | Geek touch & rare item | Use sparingly; do not turn it childish |
| Dark palette | Digital universe & premium showcase | Prioritize contrast and legibility |

### 4.3 Clear space

Minimum protection area around the logo equals the **height of the "C"** in the
wordmark, or **20% of the symbol width**, whichever is larger. No text, button,
border, icon, or image may invade this area.

| Version | Minimum clear space | Recommended minimum size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Horizontal signature | 1× height of the "C" all around | 160 px wide (digital) |
| Vertical signature | 1× height of the "C" all around | 120 px wide (digital) |
| Symbol only | 20% of the symbol width | 24 px favicon/interface; 48 px app icon |
| Social avatar | 14% inner padding | Centered symbol, no tagline |

### 4.4 Permitted variations

| Variation | When to use | Colors |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary dark** | Product, landing page, dark digital materials | Colored symbol + soft white text |
| **Primary light** | Documents, light backgrounds, print | Colored symbol + night-blue text |
| **Monochrome light** | Dark backgrounds where color isn't possible | All soft white |
| **Monochrome dark** | Light backgrounds where color isn't possible | All night-blue |
| **Symbol only** | Favicon, app icon, collapsed sidebar, loading | Colored version preferred |

### 4.5 Prohibited logo uses

- Do **not** distort, flatten, skew, or rotate the symbol or wordmark.
- Do **not** apply strong shadows, extra outlines, off-palette gradients, or 3D
  effects.
- Do **not** swap the primary purple for random colors.
- Do **not** place the logo over photos without contrast and a protection layer.
- Do **not** use gold as the dominant color of the logo.
- Do **not** recreate the wordmark with unapproved fonts.
- Do **not** add characters, mascots, or franchise icons to the logo.
- Do **not** use the symbol as an excessive decorative bullet in long text.
