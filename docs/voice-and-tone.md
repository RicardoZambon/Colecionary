# Voice & Tone

Translated and restructured from the Colecionary brand manual (v1.0, May/2026),
section 12. Explanatory text is in English; the **approved product strings and
taglines are kept in their original Portuguese** (with English glosses) because
they are literal UI copy, not just examples.

> Colecionary should speak like a **trustworthy, enthusiastic organizer** — not a
> cold corporate system. Copy must be simple, direct, and motivating.

For the verbal territory (preferred vocabulary and words to avoid) see
[`brand-identity.md`](brand-identity.md#23-verbal-territory).

---

## Voice principles

- **Simple and direct** — short, clear sentences; no jargon.
- **Motivating** — encourage the user to add to and enjoy their collection.
- **Warm, not corporate** — avoid cold/administrative phrasing in user-facing flows
  (corporate terms are acceptable only in administrative areas).
- **Geek but mature** — personality comes from confidence and delight, never from
  childish language, heavy memes, or quickly-aging slang.

## Approved vs. avoid copy

Use the Portuguese strings in the **Use** column as the approved product copy.

| Context | Use (PT — approved) | English gloss | Avoid (PT) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary CTA | _Adicionar item_, _Criar coleção_, _Publicar vitrine_ | Add item, Create collection, Publish showcase | _Cadastrar ativo_, _Inserir registro_ |
| Empty state | _Sua coleção ainda está vazia. Comece adicionando seu primeiro item._ | Your collection is still empty. Start by adding your first item. | _Nenhum dado encontrado_ (No data found) |
| Success | _Item salvo na sua coleção._ | Item saved to your collection. | _Operação realizada com sucesso_ (Operation completed successfully) |
| Error | _Não foi possível salvar agora. Revise os campos destacados._ | Couldn't save right now. Review the highlighted fields. | _Erro inesperado 500_ (Unexpected error 500) |
| Wishlist | _Adicionar à wishlist_ | Add to wishlist | _Adicionar à lista de desejos do usuário final_ |
| Value | _Valor estimado da coleção_ | Estimated value of the collection | _Precificação patrimonial_ (Asset pricing) |
